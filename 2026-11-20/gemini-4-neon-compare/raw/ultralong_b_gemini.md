1. At the latest point described in the text, the Thornbury Lending Society's code word is **larkspur**; before that, it was **heliotrope**.
2. You would have to give the Society's current code word, **larkspur** (which replaced *heliotrope*), and the parcel is kept **behind the counter of the Harrowgate post office**.
3. **35** silver buttons.
   - Start: 42 silver buttons in the parcel.
   - Captain Ashcombe removes 11 buttons: 42 − 11 = 31.
   - The jeweller returns 4 reset buttons to the parcel: 31 + 4 = 35.
