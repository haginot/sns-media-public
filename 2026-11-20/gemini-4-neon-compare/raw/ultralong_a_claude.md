1. **Thornbury code word:** The latest one is **larkspur**. Before that it was **heliotrope**, and the society's ledger records the change at Michaelmas.

2. **Collecting the parcel:** Mr. Ashcombe says anyone giving the society's *current* code word may collect it, so you would give **larkspur**. The parcel is kept **behind the counter of the Harrowgate post office**.

3. **Silver buttons: 35.**
   - When the parcel was opened it held 42.
   - Captain Ashcombe removed 11 to be reset by a jeweller: 42 − 11 = 31.
   - The jeweller later returned 4 of them: 31 + 4 = **35**.

4. **Pelham Lending Society's code word:** **primrose**.

5. **What was drawn on the folded map:** **Not stated.**

6. **Sentences mentioning a blue heron: 7.**

7. **Which comes first:** The **opening of the sealed parcel**. It is mentioned among Emma's chapter headings, before Volume II. The Thornbury code word change comes later, in Volume II, Chapter XVI.
