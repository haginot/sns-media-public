1. The key is engraved with the number **47**. Mr. Bennet keeps it in the **third drawer of his library desk** (Chapter IV).

2. According to Mrs. Hill, it is the only key that opens the **green tea chest in the pantry** (Chapter XXVII).

3. Kitty secretly hid **19 ribbons** in the chest, and **8** of them were blue (Chapter XLVI).

4. Some weeks after Lady Catherine's visit, Mary took 3 of the blue ribbons and gave them to the curate's daughter (Chapter LVI). That leaves:
   - Blue: 8 − 3 = **5**
   - Total: 19 − 3 = **16**

5. **Not stated.**

6. Chapter **IV**: "When Jane and Elizabeth were alone, the former, who had been cautious in her praise of Mr. Bingley before, expressed to her sister how very much she admired him."
